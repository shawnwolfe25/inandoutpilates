Paying by Venmo, Zelle, or Cash App? Please do this

  1. Send the exact amount for your class or package to the handle above (scan the code or tap the button).

  3. Euna reviews these payments by hand and will confirm your spot. If you don't hear back within a day, just text (618) 409-4966.

Safety tip: Only ever send to the exact handles shown on this page. We will never message you asking for a payment to a different name or account.
