What we offer

Class types

Private & Duet

1:1 Machine & Duet Sessions

Personalized sessions on our reformers and Cadillac. Train solo or with a partner — fully customized to your goals. Available online via Zoom if you have your own machine.

Group

Machine Group Classes

Energizing small-group reformer classes that build full-body strength, flexibility, and control in a supportive setting.

A dynamic mat-based class using the ball and bar. Great for all levels and available online via Zoom from anywhere.
